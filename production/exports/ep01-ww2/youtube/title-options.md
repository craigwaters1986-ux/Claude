### Recommendation and rationale
**Option 1 — "World War II Explained: How It Started, How It Ended".**
- Keeps the user's working title (so no change of identity for the episode) while front-loading the primary keyword.
- Answers both halves of the two most common question-style searches observed (how it started / how it ended), and it is exactly what the episode delivers — no exaggeration.
- "World War II" in the title for broad reach; "Second World War" goes in the first lines of the description and stays in the narration (British English), so both forms are covered.
- 52 characters: unlikely to be cut off on most surfaces (the main words are in the first ~40 characters either way).
- If the user prefers the tagline, option 5 is the best alternative; for A/B testing (if the user chooses to use YouTube's test feature), options 1 and 3 are the cleanest pair.

Avoid: "The REAL reason…", "What they didn't tell you…", "Hitler's…" as the lead word, or anything using the Holocaust or atomic bombings as bait. These would mislead or clash with the channel's ethics rules.
