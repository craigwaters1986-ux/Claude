- Contrast: dark band against pale map; the single red arrow is the only saturated colour.

The 0:00 and 2:30 previews are dark and unsuitable. The map and figure need to be rebuilt as a still by the animator/environment designer.

## Concept B: "1939 → 1945" (2 tokens)
- Split frame. Left half muted grey-blue: a stick figure reading a newspaper (no legible headline). Right half warm cream: a small crowd of stick figures with raised arms (celebration mood, no flags carrying symbols).
- Dates in very large white bold type across the middle with a thick black outline, arrow between them.
