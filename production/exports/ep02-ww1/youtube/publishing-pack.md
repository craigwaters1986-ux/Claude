# Episode 2 · WW1 - From Start to Finish

Category: Education
Language and subtitles: English (United Kingdom)

## YouTube description

How did two shots in Sarajevo lead to a war that reached across the world?

This animated documentary follows the First World War from the July Crisis to the 1918 Armistice and the peace that followed. It looks at life in the trenches and on the home front, the war beyond the Western Front, and the choices that shaped its outcome.
