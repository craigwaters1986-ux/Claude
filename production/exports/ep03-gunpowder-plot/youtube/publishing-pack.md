Category: Education
Language and subtitles: English (United Kingdom)
Length: 12:00

## YouTube description

Whoever wrote it wanted it burnt. Four hundred years later, you can still read it.

In October 1605 an unsigned letter warned Lord Monteagle to stay away from the opening of Parliament. This animated documentary follows that letter from a house in Hoxton to the king’s chief minister, the search beneath Westminster and the man who called himself John Johnson. It looks at what the surviving records show, what they cannot tell us about who wrote the letter, what the plot meant for England’s Catholics, and how the Fifth of November became a date almost every child in Britain knows.
