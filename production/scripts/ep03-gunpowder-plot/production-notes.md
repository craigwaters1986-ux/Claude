# Episode 3 • Production notes

**Picture and sound.** Use Episode 2’s expressive stick-figure rigs and poses, flat muted palette, ink outlines, drifting camera, chapter cards and subtitle treatment. Dress identifiable people in early Stuart clothing and label them at first appearance. Depict the old Palace of Westminster. Carry the same ChannelLogo component over all 21,600 frames at x 32, y 28, width 104. Reserve the upper-left corner and check map names against the caption area. Use the original British Kokoro bm_george voice, starting from Episode 2’s 0.96 narration speed. Time captions against the recorded words. The script’s times are provisional until voiced.

**Recurring images.** Use three different pieces of paper to tell three parts of the story. The anonymous letter starts in a hand, the clerk’s note appears in the margin of an open book, and the thanksgiving law becomes a page read aloud to a congregation. Dates and locations anchor the transitions. Use a quiet page turn to bring the viewer back to the letter in the final minute.
