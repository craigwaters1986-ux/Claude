## Changes to inputs / assumptions (deviations from the storyboard)
- **S03-02:** the reverse angle cuts on the first report SFX (126.95 s), not 127.2 s, so the picture lands on the sound. The crowd's recoil is `cower` mixed at 75 % on figures seen from behind, because `Crowd` has no cower action.
- **S03-03:** I drew the cheque as a small prop inside `S03.tsx`; the library `Document` cannot show an empty amount box. I added a ticking wall clock to match the clock-tick SFX.
- **S03-06:** this shot ends at local 1716 (167.2 s) instead of the beat midpoint 1704, closer to the storyboard's 167.7 s. That gives the full-size newspaper about 1.6 s on screen. The clock tower is drawn in the scene, not built from `Building`. The newspaper's date line is a sub-line, because `Newspaper`'s date text is tiny.
- **S03-08:** **A DEBATE** is a top-right `FactPill` rather than a `TermCard`, because `TermCard` needs a meaning line and §3 gives none. It leaves at 2055, before the right panel's head appears. The book covers read FISCHER 1961 and CLARK 2012, with the full names as captions underneath, because `BookCard` is too narrow for full names. The right panel begins on the "Others stress…" caption (local 2063, 178.8 s), not at 180.8 s. The balance scale sits at the top centre.
- **S04-01:** the "background panels" are two split-screen panels that slide in from the right at about 3.1–4.1 s; the British platform keeps the left third.
- **S04-04:** the "THE RACE TO THE SEA" label is a right-middle `FactPill`.
- **S04-06:** the Eve quote uses the kit's attributed `QuoteCard`, with the exact text, rather than `MemorialCard`. The camera pans from the centre to the left, then to the right (the storyboard's "L→R" conflicts with the burial party being on the far left). The burial party stays in place, bareheaded, after the whistle.
- **S05-02:** the floor is under water (flood 0.85 throughout), so the rats run on the firestep and parapet lanes rather than the duckboards.
- **S05-03:** the gas cloud is a small ellipse-based helper in `S05.tsx` rather than tinted `Smoke`. It reads better as a low rolling bank.
- **S05-05:** the MemorialCard is held to 320.0 s (local 1800) instead of 318.8 s, so it fades out under s06's 30-frame pre-roll fade. This is the storyboard's "slow fade out of the executions card".
