## Issues

**1. BLOCKING. Ch 5, line 44.** "he opened a speech with a line from the Bible."
- The House Divided line is not the opening of the 16 June 1858 speech, which begins "If we could first know where we are…". The notes (Q3) say only "a line from the Bible", so "opened" is unsupported.
- Fix: "he quoted a line from the Bible: 'A house divided against itself cannot stand.'"
- Owner: Scriptwriter.

**2. MINOR. Ch 1, line 10.** "had less than a year of schooling."
- This is Lincoln's own reckoning (Q10), not a measured fact.
- Fix: "had, by his own count, less than a year of schooling."

**3. MINOR. Ch 1, line 10.** The first mention of Gettysburg has no context (year, battlefield).
- This matters for non-experts.
- Fix: "at Gettysburg in 1863".
- Ch 7 (line 70) carries the explanation, but the hook should land cold.

**4. MINOR. Ch 7, line 70.** "The Bliss copy begins:" is the only narrated use of the name.
- The ch 1 narration never explains it. The on-screen label does, but the audio alone does not.
- Fix: "The copy he signed, known as the Bliss copy, begins:".

**5. MINOR. Ch 7, lines 64–70.** Sequencing.
- "Bull Run, twice" is placed before Willie's death (Feb 1862), but Second Bull Run was in August 1862.
- Douglass's White House protest (10 August 1863) is narrated before "In July" (Gettysburg).
- "Their unequal pay" can be read as referring to Douglass's sons.
- Fix, in order: write "Defeat at Bull Run in 1861, and again in 1862" after the Willie line, or just "early defeats".
- Then move the Douglass sentences after Gettysburg, or say "That August".
- And "He protested the unequal pay of Black soldiers at the White House."
- The brief's "generals" are not covered. This is optional.

**6. MINOR. Ch 5, line 46.** The Charleston excerpt ends with a full stop inside the quotation mark.
- Lincoln's sentence continues (no voters, jurors, office or intermarriage).
- Fix: end the quote with an ellipsis. For honesty, add a clause such as "and he opposed Black voting and jury service" (Q6 text).
- Same pattern at line 80 (Second Inaugural "wounds.", which is also a continuing sentence). Use a comma or an ellipsis, or just accept it as a convention.

**7. MINOR. Ch 5, lines 46 and 68.** The audio homophones "Stephen Douglas" and "Frederick Douglass" are close.
- Fix: say "Stephen A. Douglas" or "the Democrat Stephen Douglas" at first mention.
- Always use the full "Frederick Douglass". Keep both name tags.
- Also, "Cooper Union" (line 48) needs "in New York".

**8. MINOR. Ch 9, line 88.** *Our American Cousin* is unexplained.
- Fix: "a play, Our American Cousin" (not "a comedy", which is not in the notes).
- "Ulysses S. Grant" and "Robert E. Lee" (line 82) get no role.
- Fix: "Confederate general Robert E. Lee surrendered… to Union general Ulysses S. Grant".

**9. MINOR. Ch 10, line 98.** The segregation at the 1922 dedication is MEDIUM (search summary, two sources agree), as `script-sources.md` already flags.
- Fix: confirm against the Smithsonian or ALPLM page before the final lock.
- Optional: line 100, "ends with a hope" (the text says "we here highly resolve"). Use "ends with a resolve".
- Optional: colonisation could be added with "he long supported colonisation" (MEDIUM, S37) for balance. Not required.
