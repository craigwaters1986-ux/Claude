# Visual style guide — The Human Story

Reference: `style-reference.jpg` (permanent). First engine implementation:
`ep01-character-lineup-draft.png` (Episode 1 draft — awaiting approval, checkpoint 2).

## Characters
- **Heads:** large, round, pure white (#FFFFFF) with a thick black outline (~5 px at 1080p for a
  44 px radius head). Head diameter ≈ 30 % of an adult's height; children have relatively bigger heads.
- **Bodies:** single thin black line torso; thin black limbs (~7 px), round caps; small solid
  black hands (fists) and feet. No hands with fingers, no noses, no ears.
- **Faces:** black oval eyes with a tiny white highlight; thick short brows that carry most of the
  emotion (angled down-in = anger, up-in = sadness/fear); small simple mouths. Closed "u" eyes for
  sleepy/grief. Optional cartoon marks: red anger vein (only for light comic moments), sweat drop, tear.
- **Hair:** solid black shapes (spiky crown as in the reference, mop, side parting, etc.).
- **Clothing:** optional flat-coloured garment over the torso line; period identity comes from
  hats/helmets, hair, facial hair, colours and props. Everyone has the same white head —
  never use skin colour or ethnic caricature.
- **Motion:** skeletal poses (walk, run, march, sit, point, cheer, cower, talk), blinking every
  3–5 s, mouths move while talking, a subtle hand-drawn "boil" on lines.
- **Historical figures:** recognisable by simplified hair/facial hair/clothes + yellow name tag
  with role. Fictional characters (the Guide, Anna, Tom, Leo) have no name tag.

## Scenery
- Flat fills, thick ink outlines (3–5 px), minimal shading (one soft gradient for skies/walls).
- Palette: muted historical colours — military greens, browns, greys, navy, dusty yellow
  (#E3C77A), occasional red accents (#B23A2E). See `the-human-story/episode-01-ww2/src/styles/theme.ts`.
- The recurring "history room" (yellow wall, green chalkboard) comes straight from the reference.
- Sombre scenes: desaturated palette, slower motion, no comic marks.

## Text
- Montserrat (bundled), heavy weights for titles, ≥ 36 px for any fact text, high contrast panels.
- Date cards top-left; captions in their own band at the bottom; never over faces.
