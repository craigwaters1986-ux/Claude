# Episode 4 music cue sheet · Abraham Lincoln (ep04-lincoln)

Phase B, 20 November 2026. Goes with `storyboard.md`. Default: **original synthesised score** (numpy/scipy, `scripts/audio_lib.py` plus the Phase B additions in `scripts/music_test.py`). The 60 s music test (`ep04-music-test.wav`) is the sketch of cues 1M2, 1M7 and 1M10.

## Motifs

| Motif | Shape | Instruments as it grows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Lincoln theme** (original) | Hymn-like, D major, 72 bpm: rising 5–1–2–3, a gentle turn, resolving to the tonic. Two 4-bar phrases. | solo fiddle + piano (cabin) → full strings + horn swells (politics) → low brass in D minor (war) → solo piano reprise (assassination) → full orchestra + choir (legacy) |
| **War motif** | Low D-minor eighth-note ostinato (D–D–F–D–A–D–F–E, Bb under the D on alternate bars), side drum, bass-drum heartbeat | cellos/basses → + brass + timpani |
| **Freedom line** | Rising string line (scale up a sixth) over the theme's harmony | violins, then choir "ah" |

## Cues

## SFX (restrained, synthesised)

Axe chops and wood chips, fire crackle, river water and pole, wagon wheels, horse hooves, pen scratch under every `write`, page turns, crowd murmur (low), distant cannon (Fort Sumter only, soft), train wheels and whistle (funeral train, muted), clock tick (9.5). No gunshot at Ford's Theatre. No comic sounds anywhere.
