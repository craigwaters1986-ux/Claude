# Episode 4 storyboard · Abraham Lincoln (ep04-lincoln)

Phase B, 20 November 2026. For **Approval 3 (storyboard)**, batched with Approval 2 (style: the test reel and music test).
Source of words: the approved script rev1 (`production/scripts/ep04-lincoln/episode-04-script.md`). Source of facts: `production/research/ep04-lincoln/research-notes.md`. Nothing here adds a claim.

## How to read this

- **66 shots in 600 s**, each at least 6 s, as Lean mode asks. Times are targets from the script's chapter windows. In Phase C every shot is anchored to its caption phrase with `T('…')` (column "Anchor"), so the real narration timing moves the cuts, not this table.
- **Size**: EW establishing wide · W wide · M medium · MCU medium close-up · CU close-up · ECU extreme close-up · OTS over the shoulder · INS insert (document or object) · MAP.
- **Camera**: push (slow dolly in), pull, track, crane (rise), whip (whip-pan to the next shot), hold. Every chapter uses at least 3 shot sizes and opens on an establishing wide.
- **Look**: the time-of-day grade and light (see the test reel). `sombre` = desaturated grade, slower motion, no comic marks.
- **Action** uses the new engine actions by name (`chopRail`, `readBook`, `write`, `signDocument`, `shakeHands`, `openDoor`, `sitDown`, `doffHat`, `handOver`, `carry`, `pointAtMap`, `polePushFlatboat`, `raiseFlag`, `rideHorse`).
- **Text**: on-screen labels. Readable documents come only from `src/timeline/documents.json` (exact, sourced). Everything stays above y = 916 while captions show; the logo zone (x < 170, y < 150) stays clear.
- **Music**: cue numbers refer to `music-cue-sheet.md`.
- Real people carry a yellow name tag at first appearance. Unnamed people are illustrative and are never named.
- Every head is the series' white head; identity comes from hair, clothes, props and tags (style guide).
