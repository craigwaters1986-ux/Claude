# Researcher memory: recurring pitfalls

- Map polygons: always point-test hand-plotted rings against towns of known allegiance (scratch script) before approving them.
  Ep01 geo.ts errors came from straight-line shortcuts across irregular borders (Upper Silesia, the Corridor, the Vistula/Nogat).
- Molotov–Ribbentrop: the 23 Aug 1939 line = Narew–Vistula–San (through Warsaw); the 28 Sept 1939 line = Pisa–Narew–Bug–San.
  Check which one a shot depicts. Lithuania was in the German sphere in August and the Soviet sphere from September.
- Versailles: Danzig = Free City (not Poland); the Marienwerder/Allenstein plebiscite areas stayed German; Memel went to Lithuania only in 1923;
  the Saar was administered by the League, not lost; the Rhineland was demilitarised (left bank + 50 km strip), not lost.
- Japan, Dec 1941: China was held as "points and lines", so hatch it and label it approximate. Indochina = troops stationed under Vichy, not annexed.
  Hong Kong, Thailand, Guam and Wake were not yet taken.
- Britannica, IWM and EUCOM often return 403 to WebFetch; USHMM, GHDI, history.state.gov, Avalon and junobeach.org fetch fine.
  Read fetched summaries for orientation errors (left/right versus west/east).
